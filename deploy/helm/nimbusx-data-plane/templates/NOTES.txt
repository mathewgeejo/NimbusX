NimbusX private data-plane heartbeat scaffold installed.

Before the pod can send its metadata-only mTLS heartbeat, configure:
  - image.repository plus image.digest (preferred) or image.tag with a customer-approved image reference
  - config.heartbeatUrl (the exact outbound heartbeat endpoint)
  - config.dataPlaneId (the issued non-secret plane identifier)
  - existingSecret containing tls.crt and tls.key
  - explicit DNS and control-plane egress rules

This chart intentionally does not claim signed jobs or upload raw data. It only
sends heartbeat metadata over outbound mTLS until a reviewed job protocol is
implemented. The default NetworkPolicy denies all ingress and egress; add only
the endpoints necessary for the heartbeat.
